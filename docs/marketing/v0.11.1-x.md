## Thread (optional follow-ups)

1. The nine seconds were entirely one line: `await forge.merged(project)` inside a loop over every project. Nothing was slow. Twenty-one round trips were.

2. The fix wasn't caching — there was already a 10 minute cache. It was that the *first* person to open the page after the cache expired paid for everyone. Now the page paints from stale data and the fresh state arrives a moment later.

3. There's one drawing of the robot, in core, shared by the app and the site. The dashboard keeps an inline copy because it's a plain script that can't import — so there's a test asserting the two are byte-identical. Without it you get two robots.

4. I also rewrote the landing page. It had config snippets and exit codes in the middle of sentences meant to sell you something. That's documentation, and it belongs in the docs.
