I run three or four AI coding agents at once, and I kept losing track of them. Two would edit the same folder. One would shut down a server another one needed. At the end of the week I had no idea what any of it had cost.

So I built Swarm. It runs on my own machine and shows me all of them in one place, live. Nothing gets uploaded and nothing gets deleted.

Free and open source: getswarm.vercel.app
