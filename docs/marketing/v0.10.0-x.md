Swarm v0.10.0 is out.

This one is about the second person. Everything so far assumed one laptop, which is the easy case. Now there's a team daemon you host yourself: your machine forwards what it already records — claims, gates, incidents, spend — and you get one view of all of it. Your laptop stays the thing that actually runs; if the team daemon is unreachable it keeps working and catches up later.

The part I wanted most is cluster-wide claims. My agent takes a task, that claim registers upstream, and if someone else's machine already holds it the second claim is refused with their name on it. If the cluster says a task isn't mine after all, my local claim is revoked — but the worktree is left exactly where it is. It never deletes work to resolve a disagreement.

Org rules travel now too. An admin posts the policy once, every machine verifies its signature against a key pinned at login, and a policy that doesn't verify is reported instead of installed.

Aider and opencode joined the dashboard, which makes six agents — Claude Code, Codex, Grok, Gemini, Aider, opencode. Both of those report their own exact spend, so their cost numbers are theirs, not my estimate of theirs.

And the dashboard started reading behaviour rather than totals: which branches actually got merged (per model, with a cost per merged PR), a Stuck badge when a session is looping on the same failing command, and a graph of which files two live sessions are both touching.

One honest note on licensing. Everything that runs on your machine is still Apache-2.0, no telemetry, no account — that isn't changing. The team daemon is the one exception: it's source-available under FSL, and becomes Apache-2.0 two years after each release. One machine free; a second person is the thing I'd charge for.

## Thread (optional follow-ups)

- What gets forwarded is a config line, and transcript text is not in it unless you opt in — and then it still goes through your redaction rules. Off entirely by default.
- Chargeback export is by user, machine, model or task. Task means your ticket id if your backlog is GitHub Issues or Linear, so the monthly CSV lines up with tickets rather than with branch names.
- The team daemon is the OAuth client for login, not your laptop. You do the device-code dance with your own IdP and the laptop only ever holds a token the team daemon issued.
- Budgets moved up a level: an org, user or project ceiling set once on the team daemon, enforced on each machine with the same warn / ask / stop it always had locally.
- `swarm backup` is a VACUUM INTO snapshot, so it's consistent without stopping the daemon. I wanted the boring operational stuff to exist before anyone else depends on this.
