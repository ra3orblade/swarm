## Thread (optional follow-ups)

1. The icons aren't files someone drew and checked in. `tools/icons.ts` reads the robot out of `core`, rasterises it, and writes the PNGs, the `.ico` and the `.icns` itself — no image editor in the loop, so changing the drawing changes every icon.

2. The rain on the site is real rain now, over three depths of column — size, speed and brightness all moving together, because the flat uniform grid is the part that reads as an impression rather than a thing of its own. 0.27ms a frame, and it stops when you scroll past it.
