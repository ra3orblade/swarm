Swarm had a robot for a logo. It was 23×28 pixels in three shades of green and it looked like a placeholder, because it was one.

v0.11.3 has a real one — 73×87 in seven tones, and the head of it is now the app icon, the favicon and the mark on the site.

## Thread (optional follow-ups)

1. I didn't hand-draw it. I decoded the reference art, measured its pixel pitch to recover the original grid, and re-derived every colour as a scale of one brand green — so the whole robot recolours from a single value and no tone is off-hue.

2. The small icons were mushy and it took me a while to see why: they were scaled by canvas ÷ grid, which at 32px is 1.6 pixels per cell. Cells landed on two pixels or one depending where they fell, so the eyes came out different sizes. Whole-number cells only, now.

3. Also: the macOS icon was bleeding to the edge of its canvas. Apple's icon grid puts the rounded square at about 80%, and an icon that fills its square sits visibly bigger in the Dock than every icon next to it.
